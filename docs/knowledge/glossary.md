## Terms

- **Run** — one play-through, from pressing start until lives reach zero.
- **Level** — a difficulty step that rises every 20 seconds of play. It is *not* a stage or a map,
  and there is nothing to "complete"; it only changes how fast and how dangerous enemies are.
- **Scout** — the weak, fast enemy. One hit to destroy, 10 points, does not shoot.
- **Zigzag** — the enemy that weaves side to side while descending. Two hits, 25 points, does not shoot.
- **Gunner** — the slow, tough enemy that shoots at the player. Three hits, 50 points.
- **Spread shot** — the temporary power-up that fires three bullets in a fan instead of one.
  Also called the three-way shot.
- **Invulnerability** — the short period after a hit, and at the start of a run, when the player
  cannot be hit. Shown by the ship blinking.
- **Power-up** — a collectable dropped by a destroyed enemy; either a spread shot or an extra life.
- **High score** — the best score ever reached *in that browser on that device*. It is not shared
  between players, devices or browsers.
- **Scenery** — background visuals such as the starfield. Scenery is decorative only: it never
  collides with anything and never affects play.
- **Map** — one of four selectable background themes (Deep Space, Nebula, Asteroid Field, Cyber
  Grid). A map is scenery only; it never changes gameplay.
- **Ship** — the player's craft. There are six, each with its own speed, fire rate, damage, hitbox
  and invulnerability time; the choice is fixed for the whole run.
- **Hangar** — the ship selection screen, opened from the opening and game-over screens. Not
  available while paused.
- **Unlock** — a ship becomes selectable once the high score in that browser reaches its threshold.
- **Falcon** — the default ship and the original stats; the baseline every other ship is compared to.
- **Dart** — fast ship with a tiny hitbox, fires slowly.
- **Twin** — ship with two parallel cannons; wider, so easier to hit.
- **Bulwark** — slow, large ship whose bullets deal double damage.
- **Stinger** — rapid-fire ship with fast bullets, slower to move.
- **Phantom** — ship that stays invulnerable for longer (3.5s) after each hit.
